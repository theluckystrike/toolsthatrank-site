Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 August 2026. This site is operated by Michael Lip, trading as autom8, Moniuszki 16, 44-240 Zory, Poland. Questions go to support@toolsthatrank.com.

What this site collects

There is no account system, no login and no contact form. The site does not set advertising cookies and does not build a profile of you.

Third parties that receive your data

Loading this page sends your IP address and browser details to the following services. That transfer is what makes them work, and it happens before you interact with anything.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and processes your IP address to serve it.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, which receives your IP address.
  • Plausible Analytics may be enabled to count visits. Plausible is cookieless, stores no personal data and does not track you across sites.
  • Cloudflare may serve a privacy-preserving analytics beacon.

If you buy something

Payments are handled by Creem, operating as Armitage Labs OU in Estonia, acting as merchant of record. Creem collects your email address, payment details and the country data it needs for tax. Your card details never reach this site or its operator. Creem's own privacy policy governs that processing.

If you email me

Mail sent to the addresses on this site is stored in the operator's mailbox for as long as it is useful to answer you, and is not used for marketing.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of any personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to processing, or complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the Urzad Ochrony Danych Osobowych. Write to support@toolsthatrank.com and you will get an answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. There is no mailing list to notify.
