Why a site that sells a pipeline also takes tips

Hi, I'm Michael. Thank you for being here. I run ToolsThatRank on my own. I registered the domain in August 2026. Since then I have published nine free studies and calculators here, each read from my own Search Console properties, plus the open-source rules_sitemap.

The pipeline is a $99 product with its own pricing page. The studies are separate and free. A tip is for the studies. It does not buy the pipeline.

What it costs each year. Not much in money, but more in hours.

What tips pay for.

  • The domain comes first, and $5 covers about five months of it.
  • Then the hours it takes to re-pull and re-check the data when a study is measured again.
  • After that, time for the next study, measured the same careful way.

Tips unlock nothing, and everyone reads the same studies. A tip also does not buy the pipeline; that stays a separate product.
